Creator: Olli Teittinen (ollitei)
Creator profile: https://sketchfab.com/ollitei
Original source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fictional-supercar-v12-goblin-0a20e49ad5774d778567cb5c3f345786
Model identifier: 0a20e49ad5774d778567cb5c3f345786
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The creator describes this as a fictional design with a unique exterior and no interior.

Distributed adaptation: assets/goblin.glb.
Changes: converted the supplied FBX to glTF, reoriented and scaled the geometry,
removed the presentation shadow plane, neutralized the wheel axles, rebuilt PBR
materials and embedded resized textures. Added selectable paint, including matte
black and preset finishes, tinted glass, responsive brake lights, independent
wheel steering and rolling, and rear wheel lock. Brake calipers steer with the front wheels but do not spin.
The source body shape and its integrated exhaust outlets are retained.
The driving calibration and generic V8 soundtrack are gameplay adaptations,
not specifications or a recording of a real V12 engine.

Textures are recoloured/blended in the terrain shader. Landscape geometry,
road and scenery are procedurally authored for this game. Pine branch textures
and distant tree sprites are baked from the photographs credited below; bark
uses the same asset's bark photographs. The coastal routes are fictional
Mediterranean roads, not surveyed routes. The circuit layout is credited below.
The photographic HDR environment is an alpine lighting source. The sky and
coastal background geometry are authored for the fictional route.
